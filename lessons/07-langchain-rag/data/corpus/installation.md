# Aurora X1 - Installation

## Mounting

Mount the unit vertically, sensing face downward, at least three hundred
millimetres from any metal surface. Horizontal mounting traps condensation
against the sensing face and is the single most common installation mistake.

## Environmental limits

Rated IP65: dust tight, protected against low pressure water jets. Operating
range is minus twenty to plus sixty degrees Celsius. Sustained operation above
fifty degrees roughly halves the expected life of the sensing element.

## Placement and radio

Keep at least two metres between neighbouring units, and avoid mounting directly
against reinforced concrete, which attenuates the radio badly. Survey the signal
from the intended position before drilling anything.
