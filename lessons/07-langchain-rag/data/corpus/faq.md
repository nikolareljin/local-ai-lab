# Aurora X1 - Frequently Asked Questions

## Why does the status ring stay amber?

An amber ring means the unit is powered but has not joined a network. The usual
causes are a wrong passphrase, a five gigahertz band the radio does not support,
or a gateway with no free client slots. The radio joins two point four gigahertz
networks only, so an amber ring on a modern mesh network is almost always a band
mismatch rather than a fault.

## How long does the battery last?

About fourteen months at the default reporting interval of five minutes. Drop
the interval to one minute and expect roughly four months instead. Storage below
minus ten degrees Celsius shortens battery life sharply.

## What is the maximum range?

Forty metres indoors through two interior walls, and about two hundred metres
line of sight outdoors. Metal enclosures roughly halve the indoor figure.

## Does the unit work without a gateway?

No. Readings are buffered locally for up to seven days, but the unit has no
display and no direct cloud path, so a gateway is required to read anything.
