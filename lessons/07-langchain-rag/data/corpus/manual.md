# Aurora X1 Field Sensor - Operating Manual

## Powering on

Hold the side button for three seconds. The status ring pulses amber while the
unit boots, then settles to steady green once it has joined a network. A ring
that stays amber for more than ninety seconds means the boot sequence stalled.

## Soft reset procedure

A soft reset restarts the firmware and keeps every stored setting. Hold the side
button for ten seconds until the status ring flashes white twice, then release.
The unit reboots in about twenty seconds and rejoins the last network it used.
Use this procedure when readings look wrong but the unit still responds.

## Factory reset procedure

A factory reset erases stored credentials, calibration offsets, and the logging
buffer. The procedure is deliberately awkward so it cannot happen by accident:
hold the side button and the pairing pin together for thirty seconds, until the
status ring flashes red three times. The unit returns to out-of-box state and
must be paired again from scratch. Export the logging buffer before starting
this procedure, because a factory reset destroys it permanently.

## Calibration procedure

Calibration drifts with temperature. Recalibrate after any ambient shift of more
than five degrees Celsius, and at least once every ninety days. Place the unit
on a level surface, hold the pairing pin for five seconds, and wait for the ring
to settle to steady green. Calibration takes roughly two minutes.
