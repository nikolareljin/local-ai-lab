# Aurora X1 - Warranty and Service

## What the warranty covers

Two years from the date of purchase against manufacturing defects. That includes
a failing sensing element, a damaged pairing pin, and a status ring that no
longer lights. Warranty service is replacement of the unit, not repair of it.

## What the warranty does not cover

Water ingress beyond the rated IP65 enclosure, damage caused by opening the
case, and battery capacity loss from normal use. Battery replacement is treated
as a consumable and is charged separately at the current service rate.

## How to make a claim

Attach a buffer export to the claim form, together with the serial number from
the underside label and the date of purchase. Claims arriving without an export
cannot be assessed and are returned unprocessed.
